# What's new in Twitch Space 2.5.2

A big polish pass on the streamer's custom chat and dock. Nothing to set up: the Streamer Buddy System side panel and the streamer's OBS dock update themselves.

## For the streamer (OBS chat dock)

- **Custom chat looks and works more like Twitch's.** Hover a line for Twitch-style buttons: pin, reply and bookmark. Pin and unpin messages, and the pinned message looks nicer.
- **Twitch's own notices, done properly.** Subs, gift subs, raids, cheers and the rest show as calm dark cards with an icon and a big number, one color per kind. A gift bomb is one big card listing everyone who got a sub.
- **Channel point redemptions** show up in custom chat, with anything the viewer typed in.
- **A name card like Twitch's viewer card** when you click someone's name.
- **Sharper emotes and badges** on high-resolution screens, and clean line icons throughout.
- **Who's around, at a glance.** Idle or offline mods are grayed out with a small white badge, and a mod's picture leaves your bar 30 minutes after they leave chat. Waiting messages show as a small dark count instead of a pulsing glow.
- **Bookmarked and flagged lines** sit in a soft pool tinted with your own chat color.
- **Do Not Disturb holds quick info and !vanish notes too,** until it ends. !vanish notes have their own little devil icon.
- **A new quick ask:** "Can you post the Discord link?"
- The sound switch in Settings is now called **Notification sounds**, and the connection glow in the top-left corner is smaller.
