# What's new in StreamerSpace 2.6.0

## Twitch Space is now StreamerSpace

Same extension, new name. Everything you've saved stays: your phrases, notes, pins, streaks and markers carry over, and Restore still takes backups made under the old name.

StreamerSpace is also coming to the Chrome Web Store as an unlisted extension, so updates will install themselves instead of you replacing files by hand.

## Streamer Buddy System

- **Sign-in now asks Twitch for your channel's mods and the channels you mod,** so the side panel can fill its "Your mods" and "Channels I mod" groups. Nothing else is requested. You may be asked to sign in once more.

Everything else is as in 2.5.2.
