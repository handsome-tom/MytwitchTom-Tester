# What's new in Twitch Space 2.4.2

- **Streamer Buddy System replaces Ask the Streamer.** A handshake button next to the search bar (and a small one in the chat header) opens the hub in Chrome's side panel. Ask the streamer (or anyone you've both added) a quick question, see their one-tap answer, mark it seen, see who's online, and look back through your history. The streamer can use the same hub as a panel in OBS.
- Questions now wait for the other person if they're offline, and arrive instantly when they're online.
- The old question cards over Twitch chat and the little whisper-sending window are gone.
- **Mod chat:** a streamer can open a chat room for one of her tags (like Mods), so mods can talk to each other; @name pings someone.
- **Flag a chat line:** point at a line in Twitch chat and click the round flag to its left. A copy with its emotes pins at the top of the streamer's panel for 5 minutes, and the line glows in your chat colour, with your picture, for connected people with Twitch Space. The streamer or a mod can clear it for everyone with its ×.
- **One panel for chat and questions:** streamers can add `?livechat` to the OBS address for a slim bar on top of Twitch's own chat. Each connection's ring says how recently they chatted: green within 5 minutes, then orange, yellow, and white if they're lurking. Their picture is gray when they aren't in the chat.
- **Clear all notifications:** a button in the header of Twitch's notifications panel.
- **Dismiss "Save your Streak":** an × on that row in the sidebar.
- **Quick info and !vanish notes:** fast notes on the streamer's bar, for 2 minutes and 30 seconds (with a soft, fuzzy fade).
- **Stats:** a tab to switch between the people you're connected with: live status and channel info for anyone, plus subs, gifts, bits, raids, next ad and viewer records if they share from their OBS panel.
