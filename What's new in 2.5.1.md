# What's new in Twitch Space 2.5.1

More control for the streamer, and tidier lists for everyone. Nothing to set up: the Streamer Buddy System side panel and the streamer's OBS dock update themselves.

## For the streamer (OBS chat dock)

- **A fourth answer: ? for maybe.** Questions now take ♥, ✓, ✗ or ?, or a typed reply.
- **Dismiss anything with ×.** A small × in the corner of the message card clears it without answering, for things you don't want to deal with.
- **Flip through with ‹ ›.** With several waiting, small arrows either side step through them, and a quiet "1 of 3" sits under the buttons. The drop-down list is gone, so the reply box gets the room.
- **Back to chat in one click.** A green chat bubble at the top right closes whatever's open (Connections, a private chat, Settings) and takes you straight back to chat.
- **Do Not Disturb.** The hand next to it: pick **For 15 minutes** or **Until I turn it off**. Meanwhile there are no sounds, no glow and no message card; everything still arrives and waits. Click the hand again to end it early. Your mods see "Do Not Disturb" in Streamer View.
- **A gear for Settings**, instead of the sun-like icon.

## For mods (the side panel)

- **Take back your own things.** In Streamer View, your questions, messages, quick info, !vanish notes and flagged lines have an ×: it takes them off the streamer's dock, as long as they haven't been answered or seen.
- **Clear all**, just above your lists, puts everything away at once: questions to you dismissed, messages marked seen, answers put away.

## For everyone: Connections and private chat

- **Private chats live in Connections now.** The separate chat button is gone. Everyone you've added shows with their picture; click someone to open a chat only the two of you see. Unread messages show as a count on the Connections icon, and "‹ Connections" leads back.
- **Tags on the right,** with a **+** that opens a small box to add one.
- **The box you type in stays at the bottom** of a private chat; the history above fits the space.

Everything else is as in 2.5.0.
